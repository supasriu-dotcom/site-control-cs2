Site Control

A fan-made top-down tactical shooter inspired by CS2. Not affiliated with Valve. Desktop with keyboard and mouse.

WASD move, Shift walk, mouse aim and fire, right-click to scope with AWP/SSG 08, B buy, R reload, E plant or defuse, 1 to 7 switch items, Esc pause. Moving ruins your accuracy: tap the opposite direction key (counter-strafe) to stop instantly, then shoot. First team to 7 rounds wins.
